# Mono Log

Mono Log lets you think out loud. Speak, and the app captures your raw thinking, then distills it back to you as structured, scannable text. Over time, it becomes a living archive of how you've thought and what you've lived through — browsable through time and people.

The input is your voice. The output is clarity.
